Bruno Fernandes ready to deliver special message to Ole Gunnar Solskjaer after Portugal vs Norway

  • by

    Sanidhya Bhardwaj
  • 4 October 2026

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes has once again made his admiration for former manager Ole Gunnar Solskjaer perfectly clear.

Fernandes is set to captain Portugal once again when they take on Norway in the UEFA Nations League later today.

Ahead of the crucial encounter, the United midfielder was asked whether he had spoken to his former manager, who earned 67 caps for Norway during his playing career.

Fernandes said: “Obviously, Ole was a very important manager for me. He gave me the chance to play for one of the biggest clubs in the world and I was very happy to play under him.

“We haven’t spoken about this match. We speak once in a while about football. 

“I hope tomorrow I can send him a message after the game that is good for me and not very good for him.”

Fernandes rose to the limelight at Old Trafford under Solskjaer. 

He bagged an incredible 44 goals and 33 assists in only 97 games under the former United head coach.

Fernandes set to play another crucial role vs Norway

Cristiano Ronaldo stormed out of the squad in the middle of the tournament after his dispute with Portugal manager Jorge Jesus.

Despite Ruben Dias also being part of the squad, it was Fernandes who was handed the captain’s armband.

The 32-year-old is now not only Portugal’s creative catalyst but also one of the key figures responsible for leading his country on the pitch.

Portugal have made a perfect start to their campaign and currently sit at the top of Group A4 after winning all three of their games.

Whatever happens in their upcoming encounter, Portugal are guaranteed to finish at the summit of the group.

Fernandes was instrumental in their previous victory, delivering a captain’s performance with two assists in the 4-2 win over Denmark in Copenhagen.

He will once again be expected to unlock the opposition defence and create opportunities against a Norway side boasting a formidable attack.

Erling Haaland and Martin Odegaard will provide the biggest threats for Norway, setting the stage for another fascinating battle between two sides packed with attacking quality.

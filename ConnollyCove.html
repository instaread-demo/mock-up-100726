Jamie Dornan movies and TV shows: from Holywood to Hollywood

Updated on:October 5, 2026 by Ahmed Samir Review By: Ahmed Samir

What this article covers: Jamie Dornan movies and TV shows from Marie Antoinette and The Fall to Fifty Shades, Belfast and The Tourist, told through his upbringing in Holywood, County Down, and the Northern Ireland places behind his best-known work.

Who this is for: Film and television fans who want a clear guide to his career, and travellers planning a trip to Belfast and Northern Ireland who would like to see the places connected to his screen roles.

Key takeaway: Jamie Dornan is a Northern Irish actor from Holywood, County Down, and several of his most praised roles, including Paul Spector in The Fall and Pa in Belfast, are stories set in the place where he grew up.

Expert insight: “The Fall showed television audiences around the world a Belfast of ordinary suburbs, hospitals and riverside apartments, filmed by people who knew the city well. Watching a Holywood actor play a Belfast man on those streets is a good reason to go and see them for yourself.”, Ciaran Connolly, Founder of ConnollyCove

Quick facts:

  • Jamie Dornan was born on 1 May 1982 in Holywood, County Down, Northern Ireland, and attended Methodist College Belfast.
  • He played Christian Grey in Fifty Shades of Grey (2015), Fifty Shades Darker (2017) and Fifty Shades Freed (2018).
  • He was nominated for a Golden Globe for Best Supporting Actor for Kenneth Branagh’s Belfast (2021) and received an honorary doctorate from Queen’s University Belfast in December 2023.

Jamie Dornan movies and TV shows cover almost two decades of work, from a small role in Sofia Coppola’s Marie Antoinette in 2006 to leading parts in Kenneth Branagh’s Belfast and the BBC drama The Tourist. Most viewers outside Ireland first met him as Christian Grey in the Fifty Shades trilogy, but his career began, and keeps returning, in Northern Ireland. He was born in Holywood, a town on the shore of Belfast Lough in County Down. The role that made his name, the serial killer Paul Spector in The Fall, was filmed on the streets of Belfast.

ConnollyCove is an Ireland-based travel and culture platform, and this guide looks at his career the way a visitor to Northern Ireland might: film by film, with the real places behind the stories. It covers where Jamie Dornan is from, his move from modelling to acting, his main films and series, his awards and his charity work at home. If you are planning a trip, our Belfast travel guide is a good companion to the location sections below.

Jamie Dornan grew up in Holywood, County Down, near Belfast

Jamie Dornan is from Holywood, County Down, a coastal town a short distance east of Belfast. He was born there on 1 May 1982 and grew up in the suburbs of the city. Speaking to the Golden Globes in 2022, he described his home as six miles outside Belfast, connected to the city but still rural in feel. His father, Jim Dornan, is an obstetrician and gynaecologist who had also considered becoming an actor.

Dornan was 16 when his mother, Lorna, died from pancreatic cancer in 1998. He attended Methodist College Belfast, where he played rugby and took part in the drama department. After school, he went to Teesside University but left before finishing his course and moved to London to pursue acting.

People often ask whether Jamie Dornan is Irish. He was born and raised in Northern Ireland, which is part of the United Kingdom, and people born there can hold Irish citizenship, British citizenship or both. Press coverage describes him as both a Northern Irish and an Irish actor, and he has spoken publicly about his pride in making work across the island. ConnollyCove’s coverage of Northern Ireland often meets this question, because many visitors are unsure how the two identities fit together. For anyone travelling to the region for the first time, our Northern Ireland travel guide explains how the north and south fit together for visitors.

His modelling career came before acting

Jamie Dornan began his career as a model in 2001, years before his first film role. He worked on campaigns for Hugo Boss, Dior Homme and Calvin Klein, and in 2018 he became the face of the Boss The Scent fragrance for Hugo Boss.

Music ran alongside the modelling. From 2006 until 2008, he was a member of the folk band Sons of Jim, which broke up before his acting career took off. His singing returned on screen years later, when his performance of the song Everlasting Love featured in Belfast.

Jamie Dornan movies and TV shows at a glance

The table below lists his main film and television roles in order, with a note on any Irish or Northern Irish connection. It is a selection of his best-known work rather than a complete filmography.

He has also appeared in The 9th Life of Louis Drax (2016), Untogether (2018), Robin Hood (2018), My Dinner with Hervé (2018), Endings, Beginnings (2019), Synchronic (2019), Trolls World Tour (2020), Barb and Star Go to Vista Del Mar (2021) and Heart of Stone (2023). These range from a supernatural thriller and a science fiction drama to a comedy and an animated musical, so his filmography is far broader than the Fifty Shades films suggest.

The Fall made his name in Belfast

The Fall is the series that turned Jamie Dornan from a model with a few film credits into a serious actor. The BBC crime drama, written by Allan Cubitt, stars Gillian Anderson as Detective Superintendent Stella Gibson. Dornan plays Paul Spector, a Belfast bereavement counsellor and family man who is secretly a serial killer. The series premiered in the Republic of Ireland on 12 May 2013 and on BBC Two in the United Kingdom the following day, and it ran for three series until 2016.

The show was filmed on location in Belfast with funding from Northern Ireland Screen. Dornan has said that the city became a character in its own right. He also said that returning for the third series felt like coming home, since he had not lived in Belfast for around 15 years. The search phrase “Paul Spector actor” still leads many viewers back to him, and the role earned him an Irish Film and Television Award and a BAFTA Television nomination.

Belfast has hosted a run of major productions since then, which makes the city a natural base for a screen tourism trip. ConnollyCove’s guide to The Lost City of Z filming locations covers another film that used the city, and our Game of Thrones tour guide shows how Northern Ireland became a filming hub.

Once Upon a Time and his early screen roles

Jamie Dornan’s first screen role was Count Axel Fersen in Sofia Coppola’s Marie Antoinette (2006), a historical drama about the French queen in the years before the Revolution. The film won the Academy Award for Best Costume Design, and it remains the answer to the common question about his first movie.

In Shadows in the Sun (2009), he played Joe, a young man who forms an unlikely friendship with an older poet, played by Jean Simmons. Wider recognition came with the American fairytale series Once Upon a Time. He played Sheriff Graham, the town sheriff of Storybrooke, whose fairytale identity is the Huntsman. The show retold European fairy tales for a modern audience. Readers who enjoy that kind of storytelling may like our guide to the legendary creatures of Celtic mythology, which come from Ireland’s own folk tradition.

Fifty Shades of Grey made him Christian Grey to a global audience

Jamie Dornan plays Christian Grey in all three Fifty Shades films, which is why so many searches for the “Fifty Shades of Grey actor” lead to him. Fifty Shades of Grey (2015) is based on E. L. James’s 2011 novel of the same name. Dakota Johnson stars as Anastasia Steele, a college graduate who begins a relationship with the young business magnate Christian Grey. The film premiered at the Berlin International Film Festival in February 2015.

The trilogy continued with Fifty Shades Darker in 2017 and Fifty Shades Freed in 2018, with Johnson and Dornan returning as Ana and Christian. Critics were unkind to all three films, but they were major commercial successes. Their soundtracks were also widely recognised: The Weeknd’s song Earned It, from the first film, was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Song.

The trilogy gave Dornan international fame and, by his own account in later interviews, the freedom to choose smaller independent projects. Much of what he chose next brought him back to Irish stories, which is where ConnollyCove’s interest in his career really lies.

Irish stories shaped his work after Fifty Shades

Several of Jamie Dornan’s strongest roles since 2016 are set in Ireland or Northern Ireland, and they show a clear link between his career and his home. The four below are the most useful for viewers who want to understand Irish history through his work, and for travellers who want to visit the places involved.

The Siege of Jadotville tells an Irish Army story

The Siege of Jadotville (2016) is based on Declan Power’s book about an Irish Army unit serving with the United Nations peacekeeping mission in the Congo in September 1961. Dornan plays Commandant Pat Quinlan, the officer who led the Irish soldiers during the siege. The film is a good starting point for viewers interested in modern Irish military history, and it premiered in Galway in 2016.

Death and Nightingales returned him to rural Ulster

Death and Nightingales (2018) is a three-part BBC drama adapted from Eugene McCabe’s novel and directed by Allan Cubitt, who also made The Fall. It is set in the countryside of County Fermanagh in 1885. Dornan plays Liam Ward, the charming young man who helps Beth Winters, played by Ann Skelly, plan her escape from her landowner stepfather, played by Matthew Rhys.

Although the story is set in Fermanagh, the series was filmed at several locations across Northern Ireland, including Springhill near Cookstown, Tollymore and Strangford. The National Trust, which cares for Springhill, notes that the house dates from the late 17th century. Farmyard scenes were filmed at Myra Castle near Strangford, which is not open to the public, and the crew also worked around the Church of Ireland Cathedral in Armagh. Anyone who wants to see the county where the story is set can start with our County Fermanagh guides.

Belfast brought him a Golden Globe nomination

Belfast (2021) is Kenneth Branagh’s semi-autobiographical film about a nine-year-old boy growing up in the city at the end of the 1960s, as the Troubles begin. Dornan plays Pa, a working-class father who travels to England for work while trying to keep his family safe. The role earned him a Golden Globe nomination for Best Supporting Actor in a Motion Picture.

For many viewers, this was the film that changed how they saw him. It is also one of the clearest examples of a Northern Irish actor telling a Northern Irish story. It sits well alongside ConnollyCove’s guide to In the Land of Saints and Sinners filming locations, another recent drama set against the Troubles.

The Tourist moved its second series to Ireland

The Tourist, made for BBC One, casts Dornan as Elliot Stanley, a man who wakes in hospital with no memory after a crash in the Australian outback. The first series was filmed in rural South Australia. The second series moved the story to Ireland, with filming in Dublin, County Wicklow and Achill Island in County Mayo, and Dornan also served as an executive producer. Achill also appears in our guide to the Banshees of Inisherin filming locations, so fans of both can plan a single trip west.

Where to see Jamie Dornan’s Northern Ireland for yourself

The places connected to Jamie Dornan’s life and work are spread across Belfast, County Down and the counties to the west, so a short trip can cover several of them. ConnollyCove produces travel guides to Belfast and Northern Ireland, and the suggestions below link to those guides where they help. You can see this for yourself by basing a visit in Belfast and making day trips outwards.

Holywood is the easiest place to start. The town sits on the County Down shore of Belfast Lough on the railway line between Belfast and Bangor, so travellers can reach it from the city centre without a car. Back in the city, The Fall was filmed across ordinary Belfast streets, offices and neighbourhoods rather than at a single landmark, so the best way to experience it is simply to walk the city. Our guide to the Big Fish in Belfast is a useful stop along the riverside. Couples planning a weekend can find ideas in our list of budget-friendly dates in Belfast.

For Death and Nightingales, Tollymore Forest Park in County Down and the shores of Strangford Lough are both open to visitors, and Springhill is a National Trust property near Cookstown. Springhill is a short drive from Lough Neagh, which also features in our guide to the Game of Thrones Smoking Sea location. Check opening times with each site before you travel, as seasonal hours vary.

If your trip falls in the autumn, the Belfast International Arts Festival adds theatre, music and film to a screen tourism weekend. ConnollyCove’s wider TV and film locations section has more guides for planning a longer route across the island.

Personal life, family and charity work

Jamie Dornan is married to the English actress and composer Amelia Warner, and the couple have three daughters. He met Warner in 2010 through mutual friends, and they married in 2013. Before that, he was in a relationship with the actress Keira Knightley in the mid-2000s. He also shared a flat in London with the actor Eddie Redmayne while both were looking for acting work.

His most public commitment at home is to pancreatic cancer awareness. In August 2018, he became patron of NIPanC, a Northern Ireland pancreatic cancer group launched at the Mater Hospital in Belfast, with his father as its president. He has said he wanted to support a charity that meant something to him personally, after losing his mother to the disease two decades earlier.

In December 2023, Queen’s University Belfast awarded him an honorary doctorate for distinction in the arts. The university’s announcement of its 2023 honorary graduates noted his two Irish Film and Television Awards and his BAFTA and Golden Globe nominations. At the ceremony, he mentioned that his father had studied at the same university.

Awards and recognition

Jamie Dornan’s best-known awards come from his work in Irish and British television, and ConnollyCove has listed the main ones here from public award records. At the Irish Film and Television Awards in 2014, he won Best Lead Actor in Television for The Fall, as well as the Rising Star award. The same year, he was nominated for a BAFTA Television Award for Best Actor for The Fall and won the Breakthrough Award from the Broadcasting Press Guild.

He was later nominated for Best Actor at the Irish Film and Television Awards for The Siege of Jadotville, and at the National Television Awards in 2016 and 2017 for The Fall. He was also nominated for Best Supporting Actor at the British Independent Film Awards for Anthropoid. In 2018, he won a People’s Choice Award for Fifty Shades Freed, and in 2022 he received his Golden Globe nomination for Belfast.

FAQs

Who plays Christian Grey in Fifty Shades of Grey?

Jamie Dornan plays Christian Grey in Fifty Shades of Grey (2015), Fifty Shades Darker (2017) and Fifty Shades Freed (2018). Dakota Johnson stars opposite him as Anastasia Steele in all three films.

Where is Jamie Dornan from?

Jamie Dornan is from Holywood, County Down, a town on Belfast Lough just outside Belfast in Northern Ireland. He grew up in the suburbs of Belfast and went to school at Methodist College Belfast.

Is Jamie Dornan Irish or British?

Jamie Dornan was born in Northern Ireland, which is part of the United Kingdom, and people born there can be Irish, British or both. He is widely described as a Northern Irish and Irish actor and has spoken about his pride in working across the island of Ireland.

What is Jamie Dornan best known for?

Jamie Dornan is best known for playing Christian Grey in the Fifty Shades trilogy and the serial killer Paul Spector in the BBC drama The Fall. His later work in Belfast and The Tourist brought him wider critical praise.

Father Weeps in Court After Placing Toddler at a Hotel Window Before Fatal 40-Foot Fall

British father Joshua Foster has pleaded guilty in a Cyprus court after his toddler son fatally fell from a fourth-floor hotel window during a family holiday. The 37-year-old wept in Paphos District Court on Tuesday as prosecutors detailed the incident at the five-star King Evelthon Beach Hotel and Resort in Chlorakas.

The tragedy occurred on July 12, the first day of the family's vacation. While waiting for an elevator before heading to dinner, Foster placed his two-year-old son on a windowsill to show him a view of a waterpark. Foster believed the window was closed with protective glass, but the section was open, and the boy fell nearly 40 feet before Foster could react.

Defense Details Momentary Lapse

In court, Foster admitted to causing death through a reckless, rash, or dangerous act. His defense lawyer, Alexandros Alexandrou, explained that Foster was in a good mood and focused on entertaining his child when the incident happened.

Alexandrou told the court: "From that moment, the man's life changed forever." The defense lawyer added: "We're allowed, even when we're happy, to lose our concentration. And in that moment he was happy, he held his child."

The defense emphasized that Foster had fully cooperated with authorities during eight days in custody and had already suffered the severe consequences of his actions. Alexandrou argued: "This will not end with two, three years of prison. This is a punishment he and his family will bear for the rest of their lives."

Sentencing Set for October 16

Prosecutor Chryso Pergandi told the court that emergency services received reports of the fall around 6:40 pm. The boy was rushed to Paphos General Hospital, where resuscitation efforts failed. A blood sample taken from Foster approximately six hours after the incident showed an estimated alcohol concentration of 124mg, though the defense argued this had no bearing on the event.

The court heard that the window measured 2.85 meters wide and 1.2 meters high. Foster, an auditor who faces disciplinary proceedings regarding his professional license, is scheduled to be sentenced on October 16.

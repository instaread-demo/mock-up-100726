A brand-new thriller is under production for Channel 4, here’s all the latest information we have on The Night They Vanished:

A sparkling new thriller promises to bring buckets of tension to our screens with a cast announced and filming currently underway in Wales. The newest release of information has all the latest details.

What’s The Night They Vanished about?

Based on Vanessa Savage’s novel of the same name, the series focusses on a mystery that engulfs the lives of those close to it. A strange clue to a future crime left open on the internet drives a woman to unfold her own life in order to save her family. The crime is linked to her newest fling, who protests their innocence and now she doesn’t know where to turn, but she must turn somewhere as a grim countdown begins.

Who’s in it?

The cast includes Gabrielle Creevy (Amadeus), Callum Scott Howells (It’s a Sin), Tamzin Outhwaite (The Wives), Steffan Rhodri (Gavin & Stacey), Matthew Gravelle (Mudtown), Lisa Victoria (Pobol y Cwm), Jack Quick (Tree on a Hill), Andrew Lennon (Death Valley), Callum Hymers (The Pact), and Bella Shewan.

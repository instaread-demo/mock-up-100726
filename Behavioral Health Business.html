Headway Faces Backlash After Unveiling New Opt-In Patient Marketplace With $10 Per-Session Fee

By Ashleigh Hollowell | October 2, 2026October 2, 2026

Venture-backed digital mental health platform Headway yesterday announced a new opt-in marketplace offering for clinicians in response to requests to connect with more patients. For every session initiated through the new marketplace option, the company will now charge clinicians a $9.99 fee per session.

In response, multiple clinicians on its platform and others across the sector who are not part of the platform spoke out against the move on social media and directly to Behavioral Health Business. Broadly, the industry’s collective concern is that the per-session marketplace fees for clients acquired via this new opt-in choice will squeeze the providers on its platform even tighter financially on top of current reimbursement rates and other industry pressures already have.

Headway has stated that the $9.99 per-session fee does not apply to other patients that the providers bring in on their own via their direct original link.

Advertisement

“There’s no fee for clients who book through a provider’s own Headway booking link,” a spokesperson from the company told BHB. “That includes clients who find a provider on Psychology Today, their own website, or anywhere else [and] then book through that link. The fee applies only when a client finds and books a provider through the Headway marketplace.”

Existing caseloads are also exempt from the fee.

Essentially, when clinicians opt in to the new marketplace, they will have a separate, unique UTM tracking portion of the link. If they accept marketplace patients who come to Headway.co without a specific therapist in mind, the new search and matching flow function will apply the fee to each session they have with that patient.

Advertisement

Clinicians do have the option to opt out of Headway’s new marketplace option entirely and continue using the platform in the way they always have with their direct Headway link. There is also no penalty for doing so, a spokesperson for the company confirmed to BHB.

Because Headway aims to be a back-end solution for small practices, the $9.99 fee may turn some clinicians away and could lead to attrition.

Headway confirmed that it will reinvest additional revenue from new marketplace session fees to make its new marketplace and patient app offerings more robust over time. The company has already invested $150 million in its new marketplace platform and to get its patient app up and running this year alone, the spokesperson confirmed.

“We’ve invested a lot into getting this patient marketplace up to where it is, but we have 90,000 providers on the platform, many of whom want access to more patients,” Headway’s spokesperson said. “So, to drive that sort of demand and that amount of patients for our providers, we need to invest a lot more. If we could do this without introducing a new fee, I’m sure we would have. But this is to make sure that we can invest on top of that $150 million.”

Headway did not confirm the number of providers it anticipates will opt in to the new marketplace patient option with fees. The company also did not comment on how much it expects to bring in from the new fee revenue.

Earlier this year, Headway also introduced biometric verification for patients on its platform – a move that also raised concerns over data privacy risks from some clinicians.

Advertisement

The company reached a $2.3 billion valuation in 2024 after raising $100 million in a Series D round.

Headway has historically been a B2B focused company, but the launch of the marketplace and its new app are the company’s inaugural steps to become more “patient-facing,” the spokesperson said. That will be a focus for Headway moving forward.

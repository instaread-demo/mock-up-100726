DJ Suzy brings Y2K melodrama to the final Kira Krash preview, “end  scene”

"end scene" is taken from DJ Suzy's Kira Krash, which arrives October 23.
DJ Suzy by Isak Berglund Mattson-Marn
Listen to audio version of this article

Instaread

Are you also impatiently waiting for DJ Suzy’s debut album Kira Krash? The trained choral singer and composer turned producer has been teasing the project one track at a time, with “why did i say i loved you”, “speedboat” and “sw33t” blurring glossy pop, R&B, breakbeats and early digital nostalgia. Lucky us, “sw33t” even got a second life through a collaboration with Nourished By Time. Now, just a few weeks before release day, she is sharing the album’s final preview, “end scene”.

“end scene” goes straight for the Y2K melodrama. Layered harmonies, emo pop melodies, dramatic strings and video game inspired textures give the track the feeling of a closing sequence from some lost early 2000s movie or console game. DJ Suzy has specifically pointed to Super Mario Galaxy as part of its sonic DNA, while the lyrics imagine a failed relationship as a film whose script has gone off course. “In ‘end scene’, I’m looking back on a relationship as if it were a movie. I wrote a script for how I wanted the relationship to unfold, but my lover didn’t follow it the way I had imagined,” she explains. By the outro, she is watching the credits roll on the life they shared, with all the drama pushed right to the surface. “I love how the dramatic chords and strings play together with the emo-pop melodies, i’m really happy how it turned out.”

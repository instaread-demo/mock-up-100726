Analyst to XRP Investors: Something Is On the Verge of Happening. Here’s why

    XRP spent most of the last two weeks compressing into a tight range. Crypto analyst Bird (@Bird_XRPL) posted a 1-hour chart circling a critical area where price action is converging at the apex of a descending triangle pattern. The current price sits at $1.4883, and Bird’s message is simple: “Something is on the verge of happening.”

    What the Chart Shows

    The chart covers roughly September 19 through October 5. XRP surged and peaked near $1.60 around September 21. It then began forming lower highs. A descending resistance line connects those lower highs, running from the peak down toward the $1.50 area. Meanwhile, a rising support line holds from lows near $1.38 in late September, gradually toward the current price.

    The two lines converge at the apex, which Bird has circled in green. The asset is now trading directly at that convergence point, around $1.49 to $1.50. The dotted horizontal line at $1.4883 marks the current level, sitting right at the intersection of both trendlines. This is the decision zone.

    A descending triangle of this structure typically resolves with a significant directional move. The longer the price compresses inside the pattern, the more energy builds behind the eventual break.

    The Community Weighs In

    Responses to Bird’s post reflected sentiment. One community member said he hopes XRP reaches double digits and is focused on selling enough to pay off debt and holding a position. Another said he sees the wick down to $1.45 as evidence of strong buying at that level, with selling pressure around $1.55, and called the setup bullish.

    One commenter declared a breakout imminent, and another described the moment as a coin flip. Not everyone shared that optimism. One person suggested XRP would go down, and another advised investors to dump the token. A third commenter noted that nothing happens at these setups. He expects the same pattern to play out this time.

    Watching the Setup

    XRP sits at a technical inflection point. The triangle has reached its apex. The asset’s price is holding above short-term support after a sharp rejection lower. Bird’s post suggests that the next significant move is close. Whether that move breaks upward through resistance near $1.50 or fails and revisits lower support will likely determine XRP’s near-term trajectory.

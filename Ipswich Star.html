Free student-led dental appointments offered in Ipswich

Families are being invited to book free dental hygiene and therapy appointments in Ipswich.

The sessions are part of a new student-led clinic launching in October through a partnership between Lighthouse Dental Practice and the University of Suffolk, and are available to anyone living in Suffolk.

From October 1, dental hygiene and therapy students have been seeing patients at the Lighthouse Dental Practice clinic in central Ipswich four days a week, under professional supervision.

Dr Tocca, principal dentist at Lighthouse Dental Practice, said: "We’re delighted to be working closely with the University of Suffolk to provide clinical placements for the next generation of dental professionals.

"For our students, being able to work with patients is an essential part of developing their skills and experience.

"Meanwhile, for the community, it means we are able to offer a number of dental hygiene and dental therapy appointments completely free of charge."

The programme focuses on preventative care and gives students valuable hands-on experience while improving access to dental services in the community.

Appointments will be available from 9am to 5pm on clinic days, and the practice is encouraging people to register their interest ahead of the October launch.

The scheme comes as many people continue to face challenges accessing routine dental care.

Dr Tocca said: "We want the students’ appointment books to be busy from their very first day, so we would encourage anyone who may benefit to get in touch with us now."

The practice is especially keen to reach teenagers and young adults who could benefit from preventive care, though appointments may also be available to other eligible patients.

All student appointments are carried out under appropriate professional supervision.

Lighthouse Dental Practice is working with schools, sixth forms, charities, and community organisations in Ipswich and nearby areas to make sure those most in need of free dental care are aware of the opportunity.

The student placement scheme is run in partnership with the University of Suffolk.

To learn more, check eligibility, or book an appointment, contact Lighthouse Dental Practice on 01473 257379 or WhatsApp 07727 728906.

Lighthouse Dental Practice is located on High Street in Ipswich.
